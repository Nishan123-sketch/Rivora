Limits and reproducibility

Historical datum epochs, relocation records and original observation-arrival vintages are unavailable. Suspected discontinuities are flags, not verified datum changes; no raw correction or invented observation was applied. Forecast rainfall is excluded because historical issuance/availability archives were not established. Model weights stay frozen after evaluation; no refit on the final test. Artifacts carry dataset, policy, engine and checksum identifiers; the tested runtime is pinned. Exact warning safeguards and its insufficient-calibration outcome remain unchanged.

Sources: configured official HRFCO/WAMIS/KMA database observations; authoritative station IDs and point-rain links. All results are retrospective research or explicitly labelled baseline demonstrations, not operational flood advice.
